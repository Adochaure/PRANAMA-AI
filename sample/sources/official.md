# Surya Scholars Grant — Official Notice

Published: 10 June 2026
Academic cycle: 2026-27

Applicants must have annual household income below Rs 8 lakh.

The online application deadline is 15 December 2026.

Submit an income certificate issued by the Tehsildar with your application.

Apply through the State Student Support Portal.

There is no application fee.

For help, contact the scholarship cell by email.
